ESF-DT manuscript figures
=========================

All eight figures are generated directly by the model and its validation
script. None has been redrawn or edited by hand.

Fig1_furnace_schematic.png             The furnace as represented by the
                                       model. Bath levels, electrode
                                       immersion, brush arc gap, freeze
                                       lining thickness, temperatures and
                                       compositions are all drawn from the
                                       current solution.

Fig2_solution_flowchart.png            Model development and solution
                                       procedure, covering the four stages
                                       described in Section 3.

Fig3_SEC_vs_metallisation.png          Specific energy consumption against
                                       metallisation ratio for three DRI
                                       grades, with published FactSage
                                       anchor points.

Fig4_SEC_and_Si_vs_temperature.png     Specific energy consumption and hot
                                       metal silicon against tapping
                                       temperature, with published anchors.

Fig5_lining_profiles_and_zone_losses.png
                                       Through-lining temperature profiles
                                       for the five zones and the resulting
                                       distribution of heat loss.

Fig6_freeze_lining_response.png        Freeze-lining thickness and slag-wall
                                       hot-face temperature against slag
                                       temperature.

Fig7_parity_plot.png                   Parity plot of predicted against
                                       published specific energy, with the
                                       10 per cent band shaded.

Fig8_dynamic_campaign.png              Twelve-panel trajectory dashboard for
                                       the seven-hour transient campaign.

Regeneration
------------
Figures 1 and 3 to 8 are produced by run_validation.py, distributed with
the model. Figure 2 is produced by make_flowchart.py in this folder.

All plant references are anonymised as Industry-X.
